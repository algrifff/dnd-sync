# Compendium

A note-taking app for tabletop roleplaying players. The table's Notes form a collective brain: everyone writes them together and links them up, so knowledge of the World accumulates as the party slowly uncovers it.

## Spaces

**World**:
The shared space a table of people is invited into, holding everything for one setting.
_Avoid_: Group, workspace, vault

**Campaign**:
One storyline played within a World. A World can hold many Campaigns, and anything shared between them belongs to the World itself.
_Avoid_: Game, adventure (when meaning the whole storyline)

## People

**Owner**:
The person who runs a World and can see everything in it, including Private notes.
_Avoid_: Admin, DM

**Co-GM**:
A person who helps run the game. They can see Secret notes but not the Owner's Private notes.
_Avoid_: Editor, DM

**GM**:
The collective term for the Owner and Co-GMs, i.e. whoever is running the game.
_Avoid_: DM, Dungeon Master

**Player**:
A person who plays in a World. They can read anything not hidden from them, change their own Characters, and write Notes of their own.
_Avoid_: Viewer, member

## Notes

**Note**:
Any page in a World. Characters, People, Creatures, Items, Locations, Lore and Sessions are all kinds of Note.
_Avoid_: Entity, document, page, file

**Kind**:
What sort of thing a Note describes, which determines how it is presented.
_Avoid_: Type, category

**Character**:
A player character. It belongs to the person who plays it rather than to any World, and is brought into each Campaign it takes part in. A person has at most one active Character per World.
_Avoid_: PC (except casually), NPC, ally

**Person**:
Any non-player character, whether friendly, neutral or hostile.
_Avoid_: NPC (except casually), ally, villain

**Creature**:
A monster or beast, as opposed to a Person.
_Avoid_: Monster, enemy

**Lore**:
Knowledge about the World itself, such as its history, factions, religions and customs, as opposed to a specific person, place or thing.
_Avoid_: Background, worldbuilding

**Quest**:
An objective the party is pursuing within a Campaign.
_Avoid_: Mission, task, job

**Sheet**:
The structured game statistics attached to a Note, such as hit points, armour class and ability scores.
_Avoid_: Stat block, frontmatter

## Visibility

**Secret**:
A Note hidden from Players. GMs can see it.
_Avoid_: DM-only, hidden

**Private**:
A Note visible only to the Owner until it is revealed.
_Avoid_: GM-only, GM mode note

**Reveal**:
To make a Private Note visible to everyone in the World, either by moving it or by publishing a copy and keeping the private original.
_Avoid_: Promote, publish

## Play

**Session**:
One sitting of play within exactly one Campaign, together with the log the table writes during it. Anyone at the table can open or close a Session. A Session is either Open (being played) or Closed (wrapped up at least once). A Closed Session's log can still be edited and wrapped up again.
_Avoid_: Game night, log, episode

**Wrap-up**:
Folding a Session's log into the rest of the World. Closing a Session triggers a Wrap-up, which:
- proposes additions to People, Creatures, Locations, Items, Quests and Lore;
- links the log to every Note it mentions;
- sends Suggestions to Characters.

It only ever adds History entries and never rewrites what people have written. It asks rather than guesses when a mention might match an existing Note. Whoever closed the Session reviews the proposed changes before they are applied, and running it again only proposes what is new.
_Avoid_: Extraction, processing, end-session pass

**History**:
The dated, Session-by-Session record on a Note of what happened to or was learned about it. Each entry links to its Session.
_Avoid_: Timeline, appearances, changelog

**Suggestion**:
A change that a Wrap-up proposes for a Character, covering its story and inventory and never its game stats. It waits for that Character's Player to accept or dismiss it.
_Avoid_: Pending edit, proposal
